# Vic’s Quest — Roadmap

Vic’s Quest is an optional, short path through Victor’s portfolio. Keep the game quick, readable, and useful as a CV.

## Recently implemented

- Seven themed chapters with concise descriptions and portfolio topics.
- A touch joystick with analog movement, plus separate attack, weapon, and reset controls.
- Three selectable field notes in each chapter. Each choice reveals a short example and is saved for the ending.
- A personalized completion summary with links to projects, the PDF CV, email, and home page.
- Compact mobile HUD cards, safe-area spacing, and landscape layouts. Touch controls no longer force fullscreen.
- Chapter progress, health, enemy counts, castle color themes, and gate unlock feedback.

## Next improvements

1. **Mobile visual QA:** Check current iOS Safari and Android Chrome at narrow portrait sizes and short landscape heights. Adjust only where real devices still show overlap or accidental browser zoom.
2. **Project evidence:** Replace generic field-note examples with verified project links and one concrete result per choice.
3. **Chapter objectives:** Add one quick, subject-specific interaction per chapter, reusing the existing world and combat systems.
4. **Combat polish:** Review attack-range visibility, enemy hit/death cues, gate animation, and total encounter time.
5. **Accessibility:** Add a non-combat way to browse all chapters, test keyboard-only selection, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Guardrails

- Keep a full playthrough under five minutes.
- Keep choices to three per chapter and text to a few short sentences.
- Make portfolio evidence more prominent than combat difficulty.
- Keep the main site and PDF CV easy to reach without playing.
- Support keyboard, touch, portrait, and landscape use.
