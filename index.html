  • Product scope and architecture
  • Core user journey and integrations
  • Deployment, documentation, and handover

Starting point: a focused MVP with clear acceptance criteria.

02

Automate a workflow

For small teams spending too much time moving information between people and tools.

  • Workflow mapping and integration
  • Research, content, or internal operations
  • Human review and monitoring where needed

Starting point: one workflow, with an agreed measure of success.

  • Launch and distribution planning
